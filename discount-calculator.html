Our free discount calculator helps you quickly find the discount amount, money saved and final sale price of an item.

Simply enter the original price and discount percentage to calculate the final price instantly.

How to Calculate a Discount

To calculate a discount, multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100.

For example, if an item costs $500 and has a 20% discount:

Discount = $500 × 20 ÷ 100 = $100

Final Price = $500 − $100 = $400

How to Calculate a Discount

First calculate the discount amount using this formula:

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Then subtract the discount from the original price:

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

If an item costs $500 and has a 20% discount:

Discount = 500 × 20 ÷ 100 = $100

Final Price = 500 − 100 = $400
