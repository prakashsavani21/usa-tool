Free Online Currency Converter

Our currency converter helps you convert one currency into another using current exchange-rate data. Enter an amount, choose your currencies, and click Convert Currency.

You can use this tool for travel planning, international shopping, online payments, business calculations and general currency conversions.

How to Use the Currency Converter

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Select the currency you are converting from.
  3. Select the currency you want to convert to.
  4. Click the Convert Currency button.
  5. View the converted amount and exchange rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this currency converter free?

Yes. The currency converter is free to use.

Does the converter use live exchange rates?

The converter requests the latest available exchange-rate data when you perform a conversion.

Can I convert USD to INR?

Yes. USD to INR and INR to USD conversions are supported.

Are currency exchange rates always the same?

No. Exchange rates can change over time. Banks, payment providers and currency exchanges may also apply their own fees or rates.

Can I use this tool on my phone?

Yes. The converter is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.
