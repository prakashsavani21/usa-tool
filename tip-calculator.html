Free Online Tip Calculator

Our free tip calculator helps you calculate the tip amount, total restaurant bill and cost per person.

Enter your bill amount, choose a tip percentage and enter the number of people sharing the bill. The calculator will instantly show the results.

How to Calculate a Tip

To calculate a tip, multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage and divide by 100.

For example, a 20% tip on a $50 bill is $10. The total bill is therefore $60.
