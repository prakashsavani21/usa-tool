Our free tip calculator helps you quickly calculate the tip amount, total restaurant bill and the amount each person should pay. Simply enter your bill amount, choose a tip percentage and enter the number of people sharing the bill.

How to Calculate a Tip

The basic formula is:

Tip = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

For example, if your restaurant bill is $100 and you want to leave an 18% tip, the tip is $18 and your total bill is $118.

Tip Calculator Example

For a $100 bill with an 18% tip:

  • Bill: $100
  • Tip: $18
  • Total: $118
  • 2 people: $59 per person

Frequently Asked Questions

How much tip should I leave in the USA?

The appropriate tip can depend on the service and situation. Many restaurant customers commonly choose percentages such as 15%, 18%, 20% or more.

How do I split a restaurant bill?

Enter the total bill and the number of people in the calculator. The calculator will show the amount each person should pay.

Yes. This tip calculator is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.
