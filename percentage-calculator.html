How to Calculate a Percentage

The basic percentage formula is:

Percentage Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: What is 20% of 500?

20 ÷ 100 × 500 = 100

Therefore, 20% of 500 is 100.

Example: What is 15% of 200?

15 ÷ 100 × 200 = 30

Therefore, 15% of 200 is 30.
