How to Calculate an Average

To calculate an average, add all the numbers together and divide the total by the number of values.

For example, the average of 10, 20 and 30 is:

(10 + 20 + 30) ÷ 3 = 20

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an average?

An average, also called the arithmetic mean, is calculated by adding all values and dividing the sum by the number of values.

How do I calculate the average of numbers?

Add all the numbers together and divide the result by how many numbers there are.
