Welcome to USA Free Tools

USA Free Tools is a free online tools website created to provide simple and useful calculators for everyday calculations. Our goal is to make common calculations quick and easy to access from any modern web browser.

Our Purpose

We aim to provide straightforward online tools that do not require users to download or install additional software. Our calculators are designed to work across phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Our Calculators

USA Free Tools currently provides calculators for several common everyday tasks, including:

  • Tip Calculator
  • Percentage Calculator
  • Age Calculator
  • Discount Calculator
  • Average Calculator
  • Simple Interest Calculator

Free to Use

Our online calculators are free to use. The tools are designed to provide quick results directly in your browser.

Important Information

The calculators on this website are provided for general informational and educational purposes. Results should be independently verified when accuracy is important, particularly for financial or other important decisions.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep USA Free Tools simple, useful and easy to navigate. We may add more calculators and improvements over time to make the website more useful for visitors.
