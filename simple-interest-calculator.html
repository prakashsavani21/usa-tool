Our free simple interest calculator helps you calculate the interest earned or charged on a principal amount over a specific period.

Enter the principal amount, annual interest rate and time in years to calculate simple interest and the total amount instantly.

Simple Interest Formula

The simple interest formula is:

Simple Interest = P × R × T ÷ 100

P is the principal amount, R is the annual interest rate and T is the time in years.

For example, if the principal is $1,000, the interest rate is 5% and the time is 2 years, the simple interest is $100.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is simple interest?

Simple interest is interest calculated only on the original principal amount.

How do I calculate simple interest?

Multiply the principal amount by the interest rate and time, then divide by 100.
